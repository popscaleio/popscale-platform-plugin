# Conversation assessment

Apply to Roleplay and Coaching briefs, input edits, saved-content and Journey
pre-activation reviews. Review goals, preparation, participant tasks, counterpart
rules, reference answers, criteria and scoring together. Findings do not authorize
additional edits, generation or publication.

## Observable evidence

Define transcript criteria through identifiable utterances. Distinguish reasoning,
intention, claimed execution and verified execution. “I contacted support” proves
a statement, not a call. Assess wording, not vocal tone, posture, movement,
button presses, elapsed time or external system work. Missing information remains
unknown; an omitted required utterance is assessable only with a fair opportunity
to provide it.

Require tools or verified facts only when available to both participant and
assessment; administrative MCP access is insufficient. Otherwise assess described
checks/decisions, without rewarding invented system results, notifications or exact
timings. Never perform external actions to satisfy training criteria.

Reflective Coaching assesses reasoning and described steps; enacted Roleplay
assesses participant dialogue. Physical competence needs separately observed
practice. Conversation success cannot verify it.

## Consistent goals and endings

Use approved company sources. Resolve conflicting preparation, reactions and
criteria before generation/activation; invent no routine or hidden optimal timing.
Make criteria attainable and give participants necessary task constraints.
Counterpart reactions should respond consistently to utterances; successful conduct
need not force agreement.

For approved threat-handling objectives, support both de-escalation with continued
conversation and justified termination with described support under the supplied
routine. Counterpart rules should respect that ending; unresolved service matters,
payment or dissatisfaction alone should not determine failure. Ending any difficult
conversation is not automatically success. Report unsupported technical completion
requirements separately; narrative instructions cannot guarantee runtime termination.

## Points and critical goals

Read maxima, calculated totals and each relevant Journey item's saved threshold;
reused content may differ. For each critical goal and relevant combination, test
whether remaining points can reach the threshold with zero on that goal. Consider
unacceptable partial-credit outcomes too.

Example: total 30, critical criterion 12, threshold 18 allows passing on the other
18 points. A smaller remainder excludes zero credit, not every critical failure.
Weights and rubric wording do not enforce mandatory-pass conditions.

Propose scoring/design changes within authorization; preserve existing values
otherwise. Follow Journey restrictions on setting `passing_score`; do not substitute
defaults for unread thresholds or invent mandatory-criterion fields. Report
unenforceable hard requirements separately as product/backend needs; leave readiness
for those requirements unresolved.

## Verify and try relevant paths

Review proposed and visible saved inputs. Report contradictions outside the edit
scope. Follow platform dependencies: Coaching input edits regenerate BOTH
instructions after the final edit. Never manually edit generated outputs; verify
redacted instructions through metadata without claiming semantic review.

Prepare synthetic cases for valid endings, unjustified termination, unsupported
execution claims, missing evidence and critical-goal scoring failures. Check expected
utterances, counterpart response, assessment and completion. Separate design review,
provenance/readiness and executed trials. Live trials require existing authorization
and a dedicated test company; drafted cases or green readiness prove no trial.

See the repository's `docs/evaluation/` conversation assessment scenarios.
